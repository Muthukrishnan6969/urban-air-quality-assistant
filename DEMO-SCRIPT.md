# Suggested demo recording — about 90 seconds

1. **Introduce the problem (15 seconds).** “Urban Air Quality Assistant helps city residents understand their air and choose a lower-pollution time for outdoor activities.”
2. **Search Tirupur (20 seconds).** Type Tirupur, select Tamil Nadu, India, and show the loaded US AQI, category, forecast validity time, and six pollutant readings. Explain that these are model forecasts, not local sensor readings.
3. **Explore the forecast (15 seconds).** Scroll to the 24-hour chart. Explain that lower AQI means lower pollution and that readings use the US scale, not CPCB AQI.
4. **Plan an activity (20 seconds).** Choose cycling and the sensitive profile. Show the two-hour window and its guidance. Mention that the planner chooses the lowest predicted AQI, which does not always mean safe air or a convenient time.
5. **Show reliability features (10 seconds).** Save the place. Click Try demo and point out the synthetic-data label.
6. **Close (10 seconds).** “Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript using Open-Meteo/CAMS data. Codex assisted with development and testing. The source and deployed app are available through the submission links.”

Use the live app at a readable browser width. Do not read out hardcoded AQI numbers; forecasts change. Record only the project window and avoid showing passwords, private messages, or other unrelated tabs. Upload separately to unlisted YouTube or link-accessible Drive.
